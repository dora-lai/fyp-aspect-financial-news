# ETF data (Tier C)

Created by `Tier_C_ETF_Extraction.ipynb`.

- `etf_prices.csv`: daily adjusted close for 21 ETFs, 1998–2023 (Yahoo Finance)
- `etf_universe.csv`: each ETF's role (18-ETF universe, SPY benchmark)
- `etf_start_dates.csv`: first trading date of each ETF; backtest window 30 May 2007 – 29 Dec 2023
- `sp500_sectors.csv`: S&P 500 companies → GICS sector → sector ETF (source: State Street SPY holdings)
- `trading_calendar.csv`, `trading_calendar_tests.csv`: NYSE trading days and the 6 test cases
