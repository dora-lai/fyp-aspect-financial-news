# Final Inter-Annotator Agreement (Task 61)

Two annotators independently labeled 100 shared headlines from the US Gold Set.

## Cohen's Kappa Results
- **Aspect Kappa:** 0.6782 (Substantial Agreement)
- **Sentiment Kappa:** 0.8351 (Almost Perfect Agreement)
- **Pair Kappa:** 0.6697 (Substantial Agreement)

## Conclusion
The high Kappa scores confirm the reliability of the Gold Standard annotations and the clarity of Taxonomy v1.0. Disagreements were resolved during the adjudication call, and the **Gold Set v1.0 is now officially frozen** for model evaluation (Task 64).
